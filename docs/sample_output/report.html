5. Holdout replay (simulated, synthetic demand)

The optimized policy re-forecasts and re-solves CP-SAT each week and places only that week's orders. The hand rule orders up to max when stock plus open POs falls to min, rounded up to MOQ, with no capacity or budget check. Lost sales, no backorders. Costs use the same rates as the optimizer.

6. Assumptions and limits

  • All demand, costs, lead times and stock levels are synthetic, generated with seed 42.
  • Lead times are fixed; real supplier lead times vary and would need their own safety stock term.
  • Safety stock assumes independent, roughly normal weekly errors. For lumpy slow movers that understates risk.
  • CP-SAT runs parallel workers under a time limit, so repeated runs can differ slightly.
  • Costs (carrying rate, shortage penalty, truck and order-line cost, budget) are my assumptions in config/corvane.toml, not Corvane figures.
